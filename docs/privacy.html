Privacy Policy

Noah_Demo · last updated 12 September 2026

Noah_Demo is a student coursework project for CPEN 321 at the University of British Columbia. It is not a commercial product. This policy describes exactly what the application does with personal information.

What the app receives

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives your first name, last name, and email address from Google's sign-in service. It requests no other scopes and cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google service.

How it is used

Your name is displayed on screen, in the app, on your own device, so that the application can show who is signed in. That is the only use.

What is stored

  • Nothing is stored on any server. Your name and email are never transmitted to the application's back-end.
  • The signed-in name is held in the app's memory only while the screen is open and is discarded when the app closes.
  • The application has no user database and no user accounts of its own.

Server logs

The back-end is an ordinary web server. Like any web server, it processes the IP address of devices that connect to it in order to respond to them, and its request logs may contain those addresses. These logs are not linked to any Google account, are not analysed, and are not shared.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No advertising, tracking, profiling, or analytics of any kind.
  • No data is used to train any system.

Third-party services

Sign-in is handled by Google, under Google's own privacy policy. The "Live updates" feature streams pixel data from a server operated by the CPEN 321 course staff; that stream contains no personal information.

Removing your data

Because nothing is retained, there is nothing to delete. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
